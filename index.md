# Privacy Policy: Idle Eras: Rebuild Humanity

**Publisher:** NovaSpark Interactive, an independent game studio operated by Abdul Wahid, India
**Last updated:** October 11, 2026
**Version** 1.1
**Contact:** aw.wahid2008@gmail.com

Idle Eras: Rebuild Humanity ("the game") is made for children and families. This page says in plain words what the game does with information. A grown-up should read it.

## The short version
- We do not collect information about you or your child. The game has no account and sends nothing to us.
- Your progress is saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game removes it.
- Google shows the ads and handles the purchases. Ads are set up for children and are not personalised.

## Who we are
NovaSpark Interactive is a small independent game studio operated by Abdul Wahid in India. You can write to us at **aw.wahid2008@gmail.com**. The same address is our contact for privacy questions and complaints (our grievance contact under Indian law). We run no server for this game.

## What the game stores
The game keeps a save file on your phone or tablet. It holds your progress, your settings, a short record of what was bought, and a few recent play times so a grown-up can see how long the game was played in the Parent Corner. A small log of problems, for example a save that could not be written, is also kept on the device.

The game never asks for a name, an email address, a phone number, a photo, a location, contacts, the microphone or the camera. It has no chat, no leaderboard and nothing a player can post, upload or share. It has no analytics tool and no crash-reporting tool. None of the information in the save file is sent to us or to anyone else by the game.

## Ads (Google AdMob)
The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are optional and start only when the player chooses them. Short full-screen ads can appear at natural breaks, such as when an era is completed.

The game is set up as an app for children. Every ad request is marked as child-directed and as coming from a user under the age of consent, the highest ad content rating allowed is G, and personalised or interest-based ads are not used. Google says that child-directed treatment turns off interest-based ads and remarketing and prevents the Android advertising ID from being sent. The game does not ask for the advertising ID permission.

The Google software inside the game still runs on the device to deliver and protect ads. Google's page for developers says that this software may collect: the device's IP address, which can be used to estimate the general location of the device; interactions with the ad and the app, such as app launch, taps and video views; diagnostic information such as launch time, hang rate and energy use; and device or account identifiers. Google says this data is encrypted while it travels. Google's page describes one version of the software, and the version in the game may differ a little. We do not receive any of this information. Google handles it under its own policies. You can read them here:
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Purchases (Google Play)
Some items can be bought with real money in the Shop. Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see payment details. The game only learns from Google Play that a purchase happened. A grown-up check appears before any purchase and before any link that leaves the game. Purchases can be restored through Google Play on the same Google account.

## Children
The game is designed for families. Age groups 6 to 8 and 9 to 12 are selected in Google Play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and no part of the game asks for it. The game does not track children, does not build profiles of them and does not show them personalised ads.

Because we collect nothing, there is nothing for a parent to review, correct, export or delete on our side. A parent can see the play-time record and control the volume and the reminders in the Parent Corner, and can write to us at any time with a question. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

## Your save and what uninstalling does
This version of the game switches Android's automatic app backup off. Your progress is not copied to a Google account, so a new phone or a reinstall starts a new game. Uninstalling the game removes the save.

To start the game over, open Settings and choose Reset Game. To remove everything the game stored, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings, Apps, Idle Eras, Storage. Both remove the save file, the settings and the local logs from the device.

## Permissions
The game asks Android for these permissions, and no others of its own:
- Internet access and network state, so ads and purchases can reach Google.
- Vibration, for small touch feedback. It can be turned off in Settings.
- Showing notifications, only if a grown-up turns reminders on in the Parent Corner.
- Hearing that the phone restarted, so reminders a grown-up has turned on can be set up again.
- Keeping the device awake for short background tasks, used by the reminder and ad software.
- Google Play purchases (com.android.vending.BILLING), so the Shop can use Google Play Billing.
- A private permission that only this game can use, added by an Android support library for its internal messages (DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION).

The game does not ask for location, contacts, photos, files, the camera, the microphone or the advertising ID.

## Reminders on the device
The game can show a gentle reminder when offline rewards are ready. Reminders are off until a grown-up turns them on in the Parent Corner, and the phone then asks for permission to show notifications. The reminders are created on the device. Nothing about them is sent over the internet.

## Links that leave the game
You can read the Privacy Policy and the Terms inside the game. The READ ONLINE buttons open a web page outside the game, behind a grown-up check. The company that hosts the page can see that the page was opened, as any website can.

## Other companies involved
- Google Play services and Google Play Billing, for purchases and for installing and updating the game.
- Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), for ads.

We do not sell information and we do not share information with anyone, because we hold none.

## Security
The save file stays in the game's private storage on the device. The game does not open connections to servers of ours. Ads and purchases use encrypted connections to Google. The game asks Android only for the permissions listed above.

## Where information goes
We hold no information, so we move none across borders. Google runs its services in many countries, so information that the Google software described above handles may be processed in a country other than yours, under Google's policies.

## How long information is kept
We keep nothing. The save file lives on your device until you reset the game, clear its storage or uninstall it. How long Google keeps information about ads or purchases is set by Google. A grown-up can manage it in the Google Account settings and in the Google Play app.

## Your rights
**In the European Union and the United Kingdom.** The law gives you rights to see, correct, delete, restrict and move your personal information, to object to its use and to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). We hold no personal information, so a request to us has nothing to act on, but you can still write to us and we will tell you so. For information that Google handles, contact Google. For consent, the age at which a child may agree alone is between 13 and 16 depending on the country, and a parent decides below that age. The game asks children for no consent, and its ads are not personalised.

**In India.** Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and its 2025 Rules (some of the rules for children start to apply later), you have the right to information about, correction of and erasure of your personal data, and to a grievance reply. Parent or guardian consent is needed for the personal data of a child, and tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising aimed at children are not allowed. The game does none of these things and holds no personal data. Write to **aw.wahid2008@gmail.com** with any grievance.

**In California and other US states.** We do not collect, sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising. If you have a request, write to us.
